Workplace accommodations

Flexible scheduling, remote work, restroom access, and leave for infusions are common requests.

Practical tips

  • Renew accommodation letters yearly.
  • Register college disability services early.
  • Know your clinic fax for forms.
  • Document denied requests in writing.
  • Pair with IBDPal workplace guide.

Common questions

Must I disclose IBD to employers?

You may request accommodations with limited disclosure supported by clinician letters.

Can schools refuse restroom access?

Reasonable access should be provided with proper documentation.

Does Foundation provide lawyers?

No. It offers education; legal counsel is separate.
