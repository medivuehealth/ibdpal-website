Practical tips

  • Search Foundation locator plus your city.
  • Try one virtual meeting before traveling.
  • Ask moderators about medical ground rules.
  • Bring a friend for your first in-person event.
  • Pair groups with clinic follow-up.

Common questions

Are chapter meetings free?

Most are; verify registration for special events.

Can caregivers attend?

Many groups welcome partners and parents.

Do chapters provide medical care?

No. They offer education and peer support.
