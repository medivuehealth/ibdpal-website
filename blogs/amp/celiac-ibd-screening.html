Celiac Screening with IBD: Before You Go Gluten-Free

Gluten-free eating is common advice on social media. For people with IBD, the smarter first step is often celiac screening. Celiac disease is more common in people with IBD than in the general population, and going gluten-free for months can make antibody testing harder to interpret.

Screen first, then decide

Ask your GI team about celiac blood tests while you are still eating gluten. Some people also need duodenal biopsies. If celiac is confirmed, a strict gluten-free diet is medical therapy, not a trend. If celiac is ruled out, wheat or FODMAP sensitivity may still matter for you personally.

Diet coordination tips

  • Work with an IBD-experienced dietitian before cutting entire food groups for months
  • Watch fiber, iron, and B vitamins if packaged gluten-free foods dominate your plate
  • Log symptoms in IBDPal or a notebook so reintroduction has data

Questions for clinic

  • Have we screened for celiac with me still eating gluten?
  • If tests are negative, what time-limited trial makes sense?
  • How do we protect nutrition if I need a gluten-free plan?
