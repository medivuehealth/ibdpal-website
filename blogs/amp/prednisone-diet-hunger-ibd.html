Prednisone Hunger and Diet With IBD: Surviving Steroid Appetite

Prednisone can turn appetite into a freight train while your gut is still healing. The goal is enough protein and gentler carbs without living on pure sugar, plus a clear taper plan from your clinician.

Why steroids change eating

Glucocorticoids affect appetite, fluid, mood, and glucose.

  • True hunger plus cravings
  • Fluid retention mistaken for fat overnight
  • Sleep disruption driving midnight snacks
  • Reflux or thrush changing what feels safe
  • Bone and glucose risks with longer courses

Food tactics during a course

  • Anchor plates with eggs, yogurt, fish, turkey, or shakes if approved
  • Keep soft starch ready: rice, potato, white bread
  • Limit sugar-alcohol diet candies that loosen stools
  • Do not start extreme cutting diets mid-taper without guidance

See steroid and taper articles for medical context beyond food.

When to seek care promptly

  • Severe mood changes or thoughts of self-harm
  • High fever
  • Vomiting blood or black stools

This page cannot diagnose you. Severe bleeding, black tarry stools, fainting, fever with rapid decline, or inability to keep fluids down need urgent evaluation. See when to go to the ER and flare help.

Common myths

  • Hunger means you can stop prednisone early. Only taper with your prescriber.
  • Starving offsets steroids. Undereating harms healing.
  • Steroid weight never leaves. Many people reclaim habits after taper with support.

Questions for your gastroenterologist

  • How fast will we taper?
  • Do I need glucose monitoring?
  • Calcium and vitamin D while on steroids?
