All 12 months

  • January: National Blood Donor Month (US). Anemia education and blood health for people with Crohn's or colitis.
  • February: American Heart Month. Cardiovascular health, clot awareness, and chronic inflammation conversations with your care team.
  • March: National Nutrition Month (major campaign). IBD nutrition, deficiencies, dietitian partnership, and flare vs remission eating patterns.
  • April: Stress Awareness Month. Stress, mental health, and IBD symptom management without blaming patients for flares.
  • May: World IBD Day (May 19) (major campaign). The biggest global IBD-specific opportunity for patient education, international outreach, and nutrition resources.
  • June: Men's Health Month. Men's health, preventive screening, and IBD care conversations that often get delayed.
  • July: Disability Pride Month. Invisible disabilities, school and workplace accommodations, and IBD rights education.
  • August: National Immunization Awareness Month. Vaccinations and timing questions when people use immunosuppressive IBD therapies.
  • September: Pain Awareness Month. Chronic abdominal pain, joint pain, and tracking patterns for IBD clinic visits.
  • October: Health Literacy Month (major campaign). Understanding IBD treatments, labs, pathology language, and shared decision making.
  • November: National Family Caregivers Month. Supporting families, partners, and caregivers without taking over medical decisions.
  • December: Crohn's & Colitis Awareness Week (Dec 1-7, US) (major campaign). Official U.S. Crohn's and colitis awareness week. Not a formally designated month-long December observance. Pair with Foundation campaign materials when available.

Educational only. Not medical advice. Verify organization details before you rely on them.
