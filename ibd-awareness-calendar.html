A permanent planning calendar for timely IBD education. Four months are major campaigns. The other months are smaller educational features. Dates and Foundation campaign names can change; verify current Foundation materials each year. Education only, not medical advice.

Calendar year 2026 · Updated 2026-11-21 · Education only

Four major annual campaigns

Prioritize March (nutrition), May (World IBD Day), October (health literacy), and December (Crohn's & Colitis Awareness Week).

  • March · National Nutrition Month
  • May · World IBD Day (May 19)
  • October · Health Literacy Month
  • December · Crohn's & Colitis Awareness Week (Dec 1-7, US)

Use these months for new articles, graphics, Foundation cross-links, and community outreach. Smaller months still get a short feature or resource highlight.

Two dates to prioritize above everything else

  • World IBD Day · May 19 · Global IBD-specific week of education and outreach. Jump to May.
  • Crohn's & Colitis Awareness Week · December 1-7 · Official U.S. observance (not a formal month-long December designation). 2026 Foundation theme: What It Takes. Jump to December.

How to use this calendar

  • Publish or refresh one timely article or graphic per month
  • Link existing hubs instead of reinventing topics
  • Point readers to the IBDPal app for logging and visit prep
  • Update this page annually when Foundation campaign names change
