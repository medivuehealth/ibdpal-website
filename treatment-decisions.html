How to use this hub

Read the Start here trio first if you are on a biologic or small molecule and need monitoring language for clinic.

Then open the guides and articles that match your situation: starting therapy, confirming remission, interpreting labs, navigating insurance, or preparing questions for a visit.

IBDPal publishes this hub as free patient education. It does not recommend a specific brand or replace your gastroenterologist.

When to seek urgent care

Severe abdominal pain, heavy bleeding, high fever, dehydration, chest pain, or signs of a serious infusion reaction need prompt medical evaluation. Use your clinic on-call pathway or emergency care.
