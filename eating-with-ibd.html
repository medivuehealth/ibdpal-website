About the author

Aryan Shashi Kumar founded MediVue, a North Carolina nonprofit, and built IBDPal to make Crohn’s and ulcerative colitis education clearer between clinic visits. Eating With IBD is his Amazon nutrition companion to the free guides and articles on ibdpal.org.

Aryan Shashi Kumar founder page · Founders story on the homepage · Eating With IBD on Amazon

Who this book is for

  • People newly diagnosed with Crohn’s or ulcerative colitis who want clearer food language
  • Patients in remission rebuilding meals after flares or restrictive phases
  • Caregivers and teens looking for practical, clinic-friendly nutrition framing
  • Readers who already use IBDPal education and want a deeper print/Kindle companion
