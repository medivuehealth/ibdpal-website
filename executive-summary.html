Public nonprofit overview for patients, clinicians, partners, and funders
Who we are
IBDPal is a free patient-education website and companion iOS app for people living with Crohn’s disease and ulcerative colitis. It is developed by MediVue, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization registered in North Carolina. Our purpose is to make practical IBD education easier to find, track, and bring to clinic visits—not to sell a premium subscription product or monetize patient data.

The problem we serve
Millions of people with IBD need clear, trustworthy explanations between visits. Common barriers include:
  • Fragmented information: diet, flares, labs, biologics, school, and work advice live in different places
  • Hard clinic visits: patients struggle to remember patterns in food, stools, fatigue, and medicines
  • Uneven health literacy: medical reports and monitoring language can be hard to decode
  • Caregiver load: parents, partners, and teens need practical, age-aware education
IBDPal does not diagnose, treat, or replace a gastroenterologist. Every page is education only.

IBDPal iOS app

Privacy posture
Aggregated analytics; no sale of patient content
Core offerings:
  • Patient library: food-by-food guides, flare help, treatment explainers, labs, lifestyle, and family resources
  • Decision hubs: nutrition, foods A–Z, treatment monitoring, stool/labs decoder, awareness calendar
  • App logging: optional personal tracking to support conversations with the care team
  • Community pointers: state support directories and Foundation bridges
How the nonprofit is sustained
MediVue sustains IBDPal as a public-benefit program. We do not operate a paid premium tier that locks core education behind a paywall, and we do not sell identifiable patient-entered search text or health logs.
  • Charitable support: donations and mission-aligned grants
  • Clinical and advocacy partnerships: education collaborations that keep editorial independence
  • In-kind capacity: volunteer and founder engineering for free public tools
  • Transparent public metrics: aggregated reach and content inventory for audits (analytics.txt, content-insights.txt, content-inventory.txt)
Partner pages and manufacturer support listings are labeled as education/navigation aids, not paid clinical recommendations.
What we ask of supporters
Mission-aligned support (not a Series A pitch)

Older drafts of this page described venture-style funding, premium subscriptions, and data partnerships. Those plans do not match IBDPal’s current nonprofit operating model. Today we seek:

  • Grants and gifts that keep education free
  • Clinical partners who want patient-friendly explainers without brand control of content
  • Help expanding language access, accessibility, and evidence refresh cycles
  • Feedback from patients and caregivers through Ask and Contact
Why this approach matters
  • Patient-first: education stays free and readable
  • Evidence-aware: pages cite trusted sources and encourage clinic decisions
  • Independent editorial: treatment explainers are not a paid ranking of brands
  • Audit-friendly: public sitemaps and plain-text exports help crawlers and researchers verify coverage
