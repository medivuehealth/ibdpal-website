The Founder

Every lasting program begins with a simple standard: make tomorrow a little clearer than today.

Why IBDPal exists

IBDPal grew from a belief that people navigating Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis deserve education and tools that respect their time, their intelligence, and the complexity of life between clinic visits. Not flashy promises. Not jargon for its own sake. Clear guidance, calm tracking, and a place to return when the next question appears.

The founder built IBDPal around that standard. The vision is straightforward: honest, readable education that stays free; an iOS app that helps turn scattered notes into patterns a care team can discuss; and a website that keeps expanding so useful answers are never hard to find.

What the work stands for

Progress here is measured in usefulness. A guide someone can finish in one sitting. A state page that points to real support. A visit summary that makes the next appointment feel less rushed. A nutrition note that helps someone prepare questions, not replace a clinician.

That standard shapes every choice: write with care, cite trusted sources, keep the experience calm, and improve what readers actually need. The founder's commitment is to keep showing up for the IBD community with clarity, dignity, and practical help.

Building forward

Today that mission continues through published articles and guides, the free IBDPal app, Foundation-licensed education where we are authorized to share it, and resources that keep education accessible. The goal has not changed: leave each visitor with one more useful next step.

  • Write and review education that is honest and readable
  • Maintain the IBDPal app and website
  • Curate trusted external resources (AGA, CCF, ImproveCareNow, NIH)
  • Share ICN Creative Commons materials with proper attribution
  • Listen to community feedback through support channels and outreach
