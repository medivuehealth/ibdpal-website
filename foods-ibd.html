Can I eat this with IBD?

Single-food questions drive much of IBDPal traffic. Use this hub to jump to practical Can I eat X? articles. Tolerance is individual. These pages prepare clinic questions; they do not prescribe a personal diet.

Educational only · Reviewed August 2026 · Not medical advice

Start here

  • What should I eat with Crohn's or colitis?
    Start with a clinician-aligned overview before food-by-food experiments.
  • Flare foods overview
    Gentler patterns many teams discuss during active symptoms.
  • Track food and symptoms
    Log portion, form, and timing so patterns show up in clinic.

How to use this hub

Open the food article that matches your question. Read flare versus remission tips, then log your own trial if your clinician agrees.

If you have strictures, recent surgery, or a prescribed elimination plan, follow your IBD team over any generic food page.

New long-tail guides include pineapple, quinoa, honey and sweeteners, plant milks, popcorn, chickpeas and hummus, plus a dedicated turmeric and curcumin page.

When food questions become urgent

Severe pain, repeated vomiting, inability to keep fluids down, heavy bleeding, high fever, or rapid weight loss need prompt medical care, not another food trial.
